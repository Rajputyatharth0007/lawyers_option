# CounselPath UI direction

## Product
India-first legal discovery and access platform. The prototype helps users understand a legal issue, prepare a private readiness summary, explore neutral professional information, and access official legal resources.

## Visual system
- Background: warm off-white / white
- Text: charcoal
- Primary accent: restrained navy
- Secondary text: slate gray
- Borders: light neutral gray
- Typography: Inter
- Layout: standard responsive web application with clear spacing, restrained 8px corners, accessible contrast, and reusable components
- Avoid gradients, neon colors, decorative imagery, ratings, testimonials, popularity ranking, paid placement, success claims, and guarantees

## Compliance boundary
This is a product concept, not legal advice. Public advocate discovery, profile fields, and appointment functionality require Indian professional-conduct review before launch. Use fictional demo records only. Verification labels must state exactly what evidence was checked and must not imply Bar Council endorsement or guarantee competence.
